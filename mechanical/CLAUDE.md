### Scope

Work related to the vintage 1976 Polaris Colt chassis, fabrication, welding, and mounting solutions for modern autonomous systems.

**Responsibilities**:
- Chassis restoration and structural assessment
- Mounting bracket design and fabrication
- Actuator installation (steering, brake, throttle linkages)
- Welding (TIG for aluminum, MIG for steel)
- Vibration isolation for electronics
- Weatherproofing and enclosures

**Key References**:
- `/layer3-muscle/CLAUDE.md` - Actuator mounting requirements
- `/layer1-brain/CLAUDE.md` - Jetson enclosure requirements (IP65+, thermal management)
